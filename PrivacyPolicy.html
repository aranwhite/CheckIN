Family Pin Drop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Family Pin Drop lets members of a family group share a "check-in" — their location and the time — when they choose to. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

What the app uses

  • Location — only at the moment you tap Share (Apple Watch) or Check In (iPhone). The app never tracks your location in the background.
  • Check-in details — the time, the location's accuracy, and your device's name (for example "Aran's Apple Watch"), plus a display name you can optionally set in the app.
  • Your iCloud account — the app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to store and share check-ins. You don't create a separate account.

Where your information is stored

Check-ins are stored in iCloud, in the family group's private storage — the iCloud account of the person who created the group, shared only with the people they invited. The developer of Family Pin Drop cannot see your check-ins, your location, or who is in your family group.

When an organiser adds a watch that doesn't have its own iPhone, the app creates a temporary 6-digit join code in iCloud's public storage that points to the group's invitation. It contains no personal information and is deleted once used, or after 30 minutes.

Place names (for example "City Hall, San Francisco") are looked up using Apple Maps on your device.

Who can see your check-ins

Only members of your family group. The group's organiser decides who is invited and can remove people at any time; any member can leave the group from the Family tab.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your information — we never receive it.

Children

Family Pin Drop can be used on children's Apple Watches managed through Apple's Family Setup. A parent or guardian adds the child's watch to the family group. Children's check-ins are visible only to their family group, and no information is collected by the developer.

Deleting your information

You can delete your own check-ins by swiping on them in the iPhone app. The group organiser can delete any check-in, or stop sharing the group entirely. You can also manage or remove the app's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
